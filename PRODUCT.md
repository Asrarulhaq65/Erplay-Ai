# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small-to-medium retail business operators in Indonesia:
- **Owner / Pemilik Toko**: Full access, oversees all operations (inventory, sales, purchasing, reporting)
- **Kasir**: Handles POS transactions, customer checkout, daily sales
- **Gudang**: Warehouse staff — manages stock, inventory opname, purchasing
- **Admin Toko**: Store manager — manages products, customers, users, settings
- **Super Admin**: Multi-tenant SaaS administrator — manages tenant stores and subscriptions

All users access via desktop browser in retail shop environment (not mobile-first context).

## Product Purpose

Mini ERP is a multi-tenant retail management system for Indonesian small shops (toko kelontong, minimarket). Core jobs:
- Product master management with multi-tier pricing (Umum, Member, Rekan, Motoris)
- Point-of-sale (POS) transactions with barcode scanning
- Purchase order (pembelian) tracking
- Inventory management with stock opname
- Sales reporting and basic analytics
- Multi-tenant SaaS with subscription management

## Brand Personality

Bold, cepat, minimalis — bold, fast, minimalist.

The interface should convey:
- Confidence and efficiency (bold typography, decisive spacing)
- Speed (responsive interactions, minimal friction, keyboard shortcuts)
- Minimalism (nothing decorative, every pixel serves a purpose)

## Anti-references

- **Not** playful or whimsical (no illustrations, no emoji icons, no cartoonish elements)
- **Not** corporate-heavy (no excessive formality, no bloated enterprise patterns)
- **Not** warm-cream / sand / beige aesthetic (the saturated AI design default of 2026)
- **Not** gradient-heavy or decorative blur (avoid glassmorphism-as-default)

## Design Principles

1. **Every pixel must serve the task** — no decoration, no filler. If it doesn't help the user complete their work faster, remove it.
2. **Bold confidence through typography** — headings carry weight, numbers are clear, labels are sharp. Let type do the heavy lifting.
3. **Speed is a feature** — interactions under 300ms, keyboard shortcuts, Ctrl+K search, single-click workflows.
4. **Dark mode is first-class** — retail shops operate in varying light; the interface must be equally usable in bright daylight and dim back-office.
5. **Indonesian-first, not translated** — the interface is in Bahasa Indonesia by design, not an afterthought translation from English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 contrast ratio)
- Keyboard navigable (visible focus rings, skip-to-content, Tab order)
- Screen reader support (ARIA labels, role attributes, semantic HTML)
- Reduced motion respected (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`)
- Minimum touch target 44×44px
- All inputs have visible labels (not placeholder-only)
